Bring your footage.
We've got you.

Revive is a studio on your Mac. Drop in a long recording and it finds your best moments and cuts them into finished short-form posts in your brand's look: captions, B-roll and colour included.

How it works

Your recording stays on your Mac. Transcription, cutting and rendering run locally. You sign in with your email or Google so your brands (colours, fonts, logos) are saved to your account and follow you to a new Mac.
