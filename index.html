Good to know.

Who is Revive for?

Business owners, creators and personal brands who already record long-form, like podcasts, interviews, YouTube videos or webinars, and want far more out of each one without hiring another editor.

Do I need to edit anything?

No. Clips come back ready to post. You choose which drafts get made, and you can open any finished clip to adjust it.

Will the clips fit my business?

Yes. Revive learns who you sell to before it picks a single moment, and each project asks whether the posts are for connection, conversion or both.

Can I use more than one camera?

Yes. Drop every camera and any screen or call recording together. Revive lines them up by sound and keeps the frame on whoever is speaking.

Does my footage get uploaded?

No. Your recordings stay on your Mac and everything is made there. Your account only holds your creators' brand settings, so they follow you to a new Mac.

How do I get started?

Download Revive for Mac and create your account in the app. It's free during early access. Paid plans open at full launch.
